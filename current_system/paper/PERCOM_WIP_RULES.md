# IEEE PerCom 2027 Work-in-Progress: checked rules

Checked on **19 October 2026**. This is a cited paraphrase for planning; recheck the live call before submission. This document is not confirmation that the guide accepts WiP/workshop proceedings for the FYP.

Primary call: https://percom.org/call-for-wip-papers/
Submission portal: https://percom27-wip.hotcrp.com/
Official IEEE templates linked by the call: https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html

## Submission

- Maximum **four pages total**, counting figures, tables and references.
- Standard IEEE proceedings/conference two-column layout, **US Letter 8.5 x 11 inches**, PDF upload.
- **Single-blind** review: include authors' information. Do not copy double-blind rules from the main PerCom paper track, NCC or SenSys.
- Register and submit through the **WiP-specific HotCRP** site before the deadline.
- The call welcomes original, unpublished early/ongoing pervasive-computing research; preliminary experiments are appreciated. Activity recognition, device-free sensing and smart homes are within scope.

Use the IEEE conference template unchanged. A suitable LaTeX starting line is:

```tex
\documentclass[conference,letterpaper]{IEEEtran}
```

The proposed paper structure in the main handoff is our writing recommendation, not a venue-mandated set of sections. No special similarity-score percentage, mandatory number of references or permitted fifth submission page was established by the WiP call.

## Dates and participation

- Paper submission: **17 November 2026, 23:59 AoE** (UTC-12). This corresponds to **18 November 2026, 17:29 IST** at minute precision; aim to finish on 17 November India time to avoid ambiguity.
- Notification: **8 January 2027**.
- Final accepted version: **February 2027**, exact date still unannounced on the WiP page when checked.
- Conference: **8–12 March 2027, Goa, India**; the specific WiP session schedule is not yet confirmed here.
- In-person presentation and at least one **full conference registration** are required. Registration price was still unannounced: https://percom.org/registration-instructions/
- Accepted/presented WiP papers are included in **PerCom Workshops proceedings** and IEEE Xplore. This is not a main-conference regular full paper.
- Final-version/PDF checks may be added later: https://percom.org/camera-ready-preparation/ . Do not invent current PDF eXpress details or deadlines.

## IEEE policies relevant to this draft

Original work, correct citations and no concurrent submission of the same manuscript: https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/author-ethics/your-responsibilities-and-rights/

IEEE's published AI policy requires acknowledging AI-generated article content, including text, figures, images and code; identify the tool and explain the affected content/scope. Grammar/editing-only assistance is generally outside that requirement (disclosure recommended): https://open.ieee.org/author-guidelines-for-artificial-intelligence-ai-generated-text/

Record actual assistance accurately. These policy notes are not permission to fabricate evidence or to bypass human authors' review. Conference submission, author order, ethics/consent statements and spending require the team's current instructions.
