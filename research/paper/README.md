**Title:** From Isolated Trials to Continuous Streams: Activity and Sign-Gesture Recognition with a Single Wi-Fi CSI Link

**Authors:** Adhikkesh S K, Adithya Ajay, Amrith B, Gowreesh B, Rishiikesh S K and Nalinadevi K.

This four-page PDF is the version supplied by the team for the Group 067 TEA paper-draft submission on 25 October 2026. It describes the single-link capture protocol, held-out-participant evaluation, class-wise and continuous-story results, limitations and ongoing work.

The PDF is preserved byte-for-byte from the supplied final draft. It contains the dataset citation in its references. Publication here does not claim conference submission or acceptance. Private recordings and participant videos are not added with this document.
